# Vincibility Raid Tools 1.0.0-Beta.1

First public release candidate; there is no earlier public release to compare with.

## Changes

- **Notes:** raid and boss text notes with an editor, pop-out viewer and sharing. They open by themselves for the raid leader (optional).
- **Visual Notes:** boss plans drawn on the boss map with raid markers, role, class and boss symbols.
- **Reminders:** timed and triggered reminders with bars, text, icons, sounds and optional text-to-speech.
- **Assignments:** boss timelines, cooldown and action assignments, test pulls, a leader view and sign-up checks against the calendar.
- **Groups:** six group layouts and raid invites from the calendar.
- **Ready Check:** raid overview, personal warnings, chat report and a consumables bar.
- **Auras:** aura sounds, a raid debuff overview and co-tank debuffs, with defaults for the current raids.
- **Sync:** notes, reminders, assignments, roster and aura setups stay in step with trusted guild members; Versions shows who is up to date.
- **Backup & Restore:** automatic and manual backups of your data and settings.
- Works with DBM, BigWigs or Blizzard's boss warnings, with its own voice call-outs and countdowns so sounds play even without a DBM voice pack. Modules you do not use can be switched off.
